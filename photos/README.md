## Content found in the book (p. 30, translated from Greek)

> By decision of the Council of the Community of Gelveri, with the agreement of the Synod (the assembly of residents), in 1850 women were forbidden to appear in public wearing jewellery on every occasion. They could wear it at home. Appearing with jewellery was allowed only at formal family ceremonies, such as baptisms and weddings.

This is a strong story for the jewellery collection page.
